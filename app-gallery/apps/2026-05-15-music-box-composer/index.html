🎵 Music Box Composer

Place pins on the cylinder. Wind the key. Hear the tines sing.

Tap the cylinder to add or remove pins
80
How it works: A real music box has a rotating brass cylinder with tiny pins. As the cylinder turns, each pin plucks a steel tine on the comb, producing a note. The position of the pin around the cylinder determines when the note plays; the ring it sits on determines which note. Click anywhere on the cylinder to place a pin. Click again to remove.
