Kaleidoscope Studio

Draw freehand strokes and see them mirrored and rotated in real time. The patterns you create are related to symmetry groups — the same mathematics that describes snowflakes, crystal lattices, flowers, and Islamic tilework.

Modes:

Mirror: 2-fold reflection symmetry (like butterfly wings)

Triad: 3-fold rotational symmetry (like a trillium flower)

Quad: 4-fold symmetry (like a snowflake or window pane)

Hex: 6-fold symmetry (like a honeycomb or benzene ring)

Mandala: 8-fold radial + mirror symmetry

Tip: try slow curves for mandalas, fast scribbles for chaotic blooms.
