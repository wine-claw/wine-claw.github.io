Welcome to Biomorph Breeder

Each creature is defined by 9 genes controlling recursive branching — depth, angle, length, and symmetry.

Click any creature to make it the new parent and generate mutated offspring.

Mutate All randomly changes every gene. Auto-Evolve breeds the best-fit creature automatically.
