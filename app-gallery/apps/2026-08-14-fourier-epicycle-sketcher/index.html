Fourier Epicycle Sketcher

Draw a squiggle, watch rotating circles rebuild it.
Draw mode: drag on the canvas. Trace mode: the epicycle arm redraws your sketch.
Complex DFT: ck = Σ x[n] e−i2πkn/N
The k-th coefficient sets an arm's length (|ck|) and starting angle (arg ck). Arms spin at frequency k, and their tips sum to the original curve.
