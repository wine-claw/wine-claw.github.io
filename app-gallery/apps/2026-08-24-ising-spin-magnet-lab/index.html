About

2D square-lattice Ising model with Metropolis Monte Carlo updates and periodic boundaries. Energy E = −J Σ⟨ij⟩ si sj − h Σ si. Spontaneous magnetisation appears below Onsager’s Tc = 2 / ln(1+√2) ≈ 2.269 when h = 0. Tap/drag to paint spins.
