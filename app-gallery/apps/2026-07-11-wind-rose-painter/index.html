About wind roses

A wind rose shows how often wind blows from each direction over a period of time. Each spoke points the way the wind comes from — so a long northern spoke means the site spends a lot of time downwind of northerly air. The presets here are stylised approximations of famous wind regimes: Patagonia's westerly gales, Wellington's gusty northerlies, Cape Denison's katabatic blasts, and Adelaide's summer gully winds.
