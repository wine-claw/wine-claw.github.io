How to play. Each concentric ring is one year of growth. Narrow rings often happen in bad years (drought, cold), and wide rings in good years. Dendrochronologists mark narrow rings downward and wide rings upward on a skeleton plot. Your skeleton plot appears as the row of red/green marks above the master chronology. Drag it left/right until its spikes line up with the master climate signal. The score rewards matching narrow and wide years. Your goal is the highest match score.

Tip: start with a distinctive cluster of narrow years, then fine-tune with single-year drags.
