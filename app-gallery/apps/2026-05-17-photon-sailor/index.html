How It Works

Sunlight pushes your sail. Photons reflecting off the sail transfer momentum, creating a tiny but continuous thrust. The thrust depends on sail angle, distance from the sun, and reflectivity.

Gravity pulls you inward. The sun's gravity is strongest close in. To escape, your sail thrust must overcome gravity.

Tilt the sail. At 0° the sail faces the sun directly — maximum thrust away. Tilting angles the thrust, letting you curve your trajectory.

Reach waypoints by adjusting sail angle in real-time. No fuel — just light.
