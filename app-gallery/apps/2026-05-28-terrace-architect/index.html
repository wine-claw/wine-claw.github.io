Douro Narrow Terraces

Used in the steep schist hillsides of Portugal's Douro Valley for viticulture. Narrow terraces (~1.5-3 m) with dry-stone retaining walls allow vine roots to penetrate fractures in the bedrock. The stone walls absorb heat during the day and release it at night, aiding grape ripening.
