How crossdating works

Every year a tree adds a ring. Wide rings usually mean good growing conditions; narrow rings mean stress like drought or cold.

Dendrochronologists slide an unknown sample against a long record of known dates. When the wide/narrow pattern lines up, the tree's age is revealed.
