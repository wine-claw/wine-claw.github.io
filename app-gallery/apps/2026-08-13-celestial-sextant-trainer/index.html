How to use
  • Drag the sky (or use the slider) to bring the sun’s lower limb down to the horizon.
  • The sextant reads the angle between the two images.
  • Press Reduce sight to apply dip, refraction, and index error, then compute latitude from the zenith distance.
  • In Challenge mode the hidden latitude is revealed only after you check your measurement.
