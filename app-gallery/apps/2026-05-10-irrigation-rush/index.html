💧 Irrigation Rush

Border-check irrigation management mini-game. Open gates, fill bays, don't waste water.

How to Play

Tap a gate button to open water flow into that bay. Water advances across the field while soaking into the soil.

Close the gate before water runs off the end or too much infiltrates deep into the ground.

Fill each bay to the target depth (green zone) for maximum score.
