💡 How clouds form: Warm air rises and cools. As temperature drops, air can hold less water vapor. When it reaches the dew point, invisible vapor condenses into tiny droplets around condensation nuclei (dust, pollen, salt). Billions of these droplets scatter light — and we see a cloud.
