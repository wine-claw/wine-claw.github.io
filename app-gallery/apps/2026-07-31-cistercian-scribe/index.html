How the cipher works

A single glyph encodes 1–9,999 by combining four digit signs on one stave:

  • Units sit on the top right, mirrored.
  • Tens sit on the top left, flipped vertically.
  • Hundreds sit on the bottom right, flipped horizontally.
  • Thousands sit on the bottom left, flipped both ways.
