How to operate

1. Station mode: click the map to add a post office.

2. Tube mode: click two stations to lay a line.

3. Dispatch mode: click origin, then destination.

Capsules travel at ~8–12 m/s (real systems hit 10 m/s). Pumps keep pressure behind each capsule.
