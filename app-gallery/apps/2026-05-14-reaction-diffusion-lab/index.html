About
Gray-Scott is a reaction-diffusion system: two chemicals spread across a surface while reacting. Tiny parameter changes produce radically different natural-looking patterns — from coral reefs to zebra stripes to bacterial colonies. Discovered by scientists Gray and Scott in 1983, popularised by Pearson’s 1993 classification of pattern types α through μ.
