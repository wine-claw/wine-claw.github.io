MONIAC Machine Simulator

A hydraulic analogue computer for a tiny economy. Adjust the taps and watch money flow like water.

How it works: Water represents money. The pump lifts income back to Treasury. Taps split income into consumption, savings, tax and imports. Government, investment and exports pour back into national income.

Built after Bill Phillips' 1949 MONIAC — a hydraulic analogue computer that modelled the UK economy.
