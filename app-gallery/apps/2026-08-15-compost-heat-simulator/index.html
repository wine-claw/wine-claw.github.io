Why this happens

  • C/N ratio: Microbes need both carbon (energy) and nitrogen (growth). Too much nitrogen heats fast but can go anaerobic and smell. Too much carbon heats slowly.
  • Moisture: Below ~35% microbial activity stalls. Above ~60% pores fill with water and oxygen drops, producing anaerobic pockets.
  • Size: Bigger piles insulate heat better but are harder to oxygenate. Small piles lose heat quickly.
  • Turning: Mixing recharges oxygen and releases heat. The Berkeley method turns every ~2 days for fast hot composting.
