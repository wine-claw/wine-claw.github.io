Circuit Sandbox

Build circuits on a grid and watch electricity flow.

  • Select a component from the sidebar, then click on the grid to place it.
  • Wire mode: Click and drag from one grid point to another to draw wires.
  • Toggle switch: Click an existing switch to open/close it.
  • Change values: Double-click a resistor, LED, or battery to edit its value.
  • Remove: Right-click (or long-press) any component to delete it.
  • Simulate: Press ▶ Simulate to see animated electron flow and LED glow.
