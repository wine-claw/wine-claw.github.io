🌱 Soil Texture Wizard

Walk through the classic USDA texture-by-feel flowchart in your browser. Answer simple field-test questions and discover what kind of soil you are probably holding.

Start here

Take a small lump of soil about the size of a walnut. Add water drop by drop and knead until it feels like moist putty — neither muddy nor crumbly.

Step 1: ball test

Press the moist soil into a ball. Does it hold its shape?

Step 2: ribbon test

Try to press a flat ribbon of soil between your thumb and forefinger. Does it form a ribbon?

Step 3: ribbon length

How long a ribbon can you make before it breaks?

Step 4: grittiness

Rub the moist sample between your fingers. Does it feel gritty?

Step 5: sheen / polish

Does the rubbed surface become shiny or slightly polished?
