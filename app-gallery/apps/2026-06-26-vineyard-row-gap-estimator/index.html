Why row spacing and vine spacing interact. Vine density depends on the area each vine occupies: row spacing × vine spacing. Bringing rows closer together increases density unless you also widen vine spacing. Tighter spacing can improve yield per hectare but increases competition for water, light, and nutrients, and raises establishment and running costs.
