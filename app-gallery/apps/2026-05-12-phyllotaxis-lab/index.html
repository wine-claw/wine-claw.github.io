About

Phyllotaxis is the study of how leaves, seeds, and petals arrange themselves in spirals.

Most plants use about 137.5° — the golden angle — creating Fibonacci spiral patterns in sunflowers, pinecones, and artichokes.

Why 137.5°? It's 360° ÷ φ², where φ ≈ 1.618. This ensures maximum packing efficiency.
